Suit and rover state (pre-EVA checkout)

What the suit and rover report before egress. Capacities and rates scale with it; during the EVA the closed loop keeps learning them from telemetry.

Landing site & hub

Choose a lander, then pick a target on the map. The habitat (hub) goes where the lander touches down — the way NASA lands and then pinpoints a spacecraft.

Stations

Tick a station to include it; ↑/↓ set the visiting order. “Finish at the last station” plans a one-way traverse (e.g. rover pick-up). Every point still keeps a walk home to the habitat. The route re-plans automatically.

Science and survey targets

Priorities (AHP)

Pairwise judgements on Saaty's 1-9 scale. The weights are recomputed live with the consistency ratio; judgements with CR above 0.10 are flagged. Defaults are the team's documented judgements, not NASA values.

Consumables

Bar = used. White tick = what the walk home would need from here. Margins include the planning reserve.

Telemetry

Press Enable. The app finds the telemetry server by itself (this computer, then your network) and falls back to a built-in demo suit if there is none. Nothing to install or start.
