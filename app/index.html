Season, dust and Earth relay

Dust: the 10th-90th percentile band and the median of 13 Mars years (MY 24-36, 1999-2022) at the habitat, from the Mars Climate Database dust scenarios (Montabone et al. 2015, 2020); the dashed years had global dust storms. Click the chart to set the departure season. Relay: when the orbiters that carry data to Earth are overhead, modelled from their published orbits (pass rates and the local times of MRO and Odyssey passes are properties of the orbit; exact times need the spacecraft ephemerides).

Landing site & hub

Choose a lander, then pick a target on the map. The habitat (hub) goes where the lander touches down — the way NASA lands and then pinpoints a spacecraft.

Stations

Tick a station to include it; ↑/↓ set the visiting order. “Finish at the last station” plans a one-way traverse (e.g. rover pick-up). Every point still keeps a walk home to the habitat. The route re-plans automatically.

Science and survey targets

Advanced: route priorities

How much each concern counts when routes are compared. Defaults are the team's documented judgements, not NASA values. Each slider is a pairwise judgement on Saaty's 1-9 scale (the Analytic Hierarchy Process); the weights are recomputed live, and judgements that contradict each other (consistency ratio above 0.10) are flagged.

Consumables

Bar = what is left. White tick = what the walk home needs from here, as on the crew displays. Margins include the planning reserve.
